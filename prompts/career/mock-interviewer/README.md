# Mock Interviewer & Feedback Coach

> A friendly helper that asks job interview questions and gives clear tips to fix your answers.

## Overview

This prompt acts like a helpful manager who wants to get you ready for a job interview. It looks at your answer, tells you what you did well, and shows you how to make it sound even better.

**Best for:**
- Getting ready for job interviews
- Fixing up your interview answers so they sound clear
- Practicing answer methods like STAR

**Structure:** Modified C-A-R-E (Context, Action, Result, Example)
**Technique:** Few-Shot
**Output:** Simple feedback list and a rewritten answer

---

## Quick Start

1. Open [`prompt.md`](./prompt.md) and copy the text.
2. Fill in the blanks:
   - `[JOB_TITLE]`: The job you want (like Cashier, Barista, or Junior Dev).
   - `[EXPERIENCE_LEVEL]`: How much experience you have (like First Job or Entry Level).
   - `[USER_ANSWER]`: The answer you plan to say.
3. Paste it into your AI tool and hit run.
